About Flagship Program

Tequila Accelerator

Accelerate your entry into India's premium spirits market. We provide institutional-grade market access for authentic Mexican Tequila brands through direct government relationships, regulatory expertise, and qualified distribution networks.

Our Mission

Redefining Bilateral Trade Integration.

We operate at the intersection of government policy and private sector execution. Our goal is to dismantle market entry barriers, provide institutional-grade intelligence, and facilitate seamless cross-border operations for flagship industries—starting with the premium spirits sector through our Tequila Accelerator.

Our Vision 2030

  • Establish the definitive India-Mexico trade corridor
  • Catalyze $1B+ in bilateral FMCG & Spirits trade
  • Build a network of 500+ strategic institutional partners
Leadership

Guided by Experience

Ravi K. Tiwari

Founder & Chief Executive Officer
