What We Deliver

The Tequila Accelerator collapses typical 18-24 month market entry timelines into a focused 2-3 month accelerated execution program. By leveraging MIBC's government relationships, distributor networks, and operational expertise, participating brands gain rapid market access with reduced risk and investment.

Timeline & Deliverables

Accelerated 2–3 Month Program Structure

Unlike traditional market entry consultancies that require 18–24 months, the Tequila Accelerator delivers focused, outcome-driven execution in 2–3 months through MIBC's institutional networks and pre-established relationships.

Weeks 1–3: Market Assessment & Strategy

  • Comprehensive Market Report: India spirits market overview, tequila category analysis, competitive landscape, and consumer insights
  • State-Level Opportunity Matrix: Priority state identification based on market size, regulatory complexity, and entry barriers
  • Pricing & Positioning Strategy: Recommended retail pricing, channel strategy, and competitive positioning framework
  • Regulatory Compliance Roadmap: State-by-state licensing requirements, documentation checklists, and compliance timelines

Weeks 4–6: Partner Identification & Engagement

  • Distributor Shortlist: Curated list of 8–10 qualified importers/distributors with track records in premium spirits
  • Facilitated Introductions: Organized meetings with shortlisted partners, including MIBC-led presentations and due diligence support
  • Partnership Negotiation: Contract review, commercial terms negotiation, and partnership structuring advisory
  • Hospitality Partnerships: Introductions to premium hotels, restaurants, and bars for initial placement

Weeks 7–10: Regulatory Execution & Launch Preparation

  • License Applications: FL-I license applications filed in priority states with MIBC liaison support
  • Import Documentation: Complete import paperwork, customs clearance coordination, and first shipment facilitation
  • Label Approvals: State-specific label designs submitted and approved across target markets
  • Launch Event Planning: Brand launch event design, venue selection, guest list development, and media coordination

Weeks 11–12: Market Launch

  • Brand Launch Events: Execution of launch events in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore with trade and media presence
  • Channel Activation: Point-of-sale materials deployment, staff training coordination, and promotional campaign launch
  • Media & Influencer Engagement: Press releases, social media campaigns, and Influencer partnerships
  • Post-Launch Advisory: 3-month advisory support for performance optimization and market expansion planning
