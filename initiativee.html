Driving Growth

Strategic Initiatives

MIBC is committed to transforming bilateral potential into tangible outcomes. Explore our flagship programs designed to provide structured market entry, foster startup growth, and create high-level engagements across the India-México corridor.

01. Flagship Program

Tequila Accelerator

A comprehensive market entry program dedicated to helping Mexican Tequila and Spirits brands establish a dominant presence in India's rapidly growing premium beverage market.

  • Market Intelligence: In-depth consumer segmentation, pricing strategy, and competitive positioning.
  • Regulatory Navigation: State-by-state excise licensing, FSSAI compliance, and import customs handling.
  • Distribution Development: Direct introductions to India's leading spirits importers and HoReCa partners.
  • Brand Launch: End-to-end event coordination, B2B trade outreach, and influencer campaigns.
Learn More

02. Soft Landing Platform

India–México Launchpad

A structured soft-landing ecosystem enabling startups and growth-stage companies to seamlessly establish cross-border operations without the typical market-entry risks.

  • For Mexican Startups: Market validation, physical/virtual office setup in India, and introductions to local banking & VC networks.
  • For Indian Startups: USMCA market access strategy, soft-landing infrastructure in México, and local talent acquisition.
  • Legal & Tax Structuring: End-to-end support for entity incorporation and regulatory compliance in both nations.
  • Ecosystem Access: Direct integration with top incubators, government grants, and B2B customer networks.
Learn More

03. B2B & G2G Engagements

High-Impact Events & Roundtables

Our events serve as a critical bridge for cross-border collaboration, focusing on actionable dialogue and ensuring that every interaction translates into a tangible business outcome.

  • Sector-Specific CEO Dialogues: Focused roundtables across IT, Pharmaceuticals, Automotive, and Food & Beverages.
  • Economic Partnership Forum: Our annual flagship summit celebrating and advancing India-México trade relations.
  • Curated Trade Delegations: Strategic inbound and outbound missions for direct B2B matchmaking and site visits.
  • Policy Briefings: Closed-door sessions with government officials to navigate bilateral compliance and frameworks.
